# Appendix plan (final MIST structure, A–E)

The appendices were consolidated from eleven (A–K, about 11,200 words) to five (A–E, about 3,450 words) for the MIST
M.Sc. length guideline. They are generated by `tools/build_appendices.py` directly from the frozen records. No value was
typed by hand, no experiment was run and the test set was not read. Each table names its source record by short file
name. Every appendix table is numbered (Table A.1 …) and appears in the List of Tables. Pages are numbered A-1, B-1, …

| Appendix | Title | Tables | Content | Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Architectures, Hyperparameters and Reproducibility | A.1–A.5 | pruning units, shapes, parameters and MACs; dense references over three seeds; the equal recovery protocol; pre-registration and freeze commits; software and hardware environments | Chs 3–4 |
| B | Core PPO and Sensitivity Supplementary Results | B.1–B.2 | Phase 3 means by setting and method; normalised sensitivity vectors and the Phase-5 centred prior coefficients (with the Phase-7B structured sensitivity) | Ch. 5 §§5.3–5.5 |
| C | Archive, Reward and Recovery Diagnostics | C.1–C.3 | Phase 4 search quality and retention by variant; Phase 5 selected policies by setting and condition; Phase 6 accuracy before and after equal recovery | Ch. 5 §§5.6–5.10 |
| D | Structured Pruning and Hardware Protocol | D.1–D.4 | H0 latency calibration decision quantities; latency-table session log; accuracy-proxy validation; ResNet-8 threshold calibration and latency-room decision | Ch. 4 §4.17; Ch. 6 §§6.3–6.6 |
| E | Energy Evaluation and Qualification Records | E.1–E.4 | Phase 7A energy session summaries; the 11 recovered Phase-7B final models; dense-only stability pilots and qualification; energy-confirmation qualification blocks | Ch. 6 §§6.7–6.13 |
