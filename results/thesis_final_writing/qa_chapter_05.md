## 14. RQ1 answer

Yes, with limits.
- PPO's non-uniform allocations outperform Uniform (5 / 6, up to +31.4 pp, C10 SimpleCNN, test) and Random (6 / 6) at
  PPO's own sparsity.
- They are mixed against Global and ERK.
- LAMP is significantly more accurate before recovery: 5 / 6 in Phase 3, and 6 / 6 for Phase 4 F and for Phase 5 C1.

## 15. RQ2 answer

Sensitivity guidance improves the search in every phase:
- better-ranked policies are found earlier;
- fewer destructive policies are sampled;
- in Phase 4, this holds against conservatism-matched controls.

It does not improve the final model in any phase. One Phase-4 exploration measure was reversed.

## 16. RQ3 answer

The terminal policy fails to retain the strong visited policies: 0% top-10 reach in five settings and 5% in one,
against 95–100% for the best seen. Archive selection restores validation-rank retention (S2A: 80–100% top-10 and 100%
top-20), mainly through the archive rule itself. The test effect is mixed, with ResNet-8 losses.

## 17. RQ4 answer

- Reward design determines how aggressively PPO prunes.
- R0's optimum over-prunes fragile networks: a ResNet-8 loss of up to 7.86 pp.
- The constrained reward reduced the ResNet-8 loss to about 1.2 pp. It was mixed across settings, and its validation
  feasibility transferred to test in only 67.5% of runs.

## 18. RQ5 answer

Equal 5-epoch fixed-mask fine-tuning substantially narrows the method differences:
- PPO − LAMP falls from 0.26–0.53 to 0.00–0.22 pp, inside the ±0.5 pp CI margin in 6 / 6, with LAMP still ahead in 3;
- PPO − Global falls to at most 0.12 pp.

RQ6 is not answered here.

## 19. Numerical corrections discovered

1. **Rounding of the largest PPO–Uniform gain.** The exact mean is **31.365** pp: test accuracies in 0.01 steps
   averaged over 20 seeds give exact ties. The frozen `result_freeze.md` and `claim_matrix.md` print 31.36, which is
   binary-float formatting of 31.364999…. The same frozen files round the other exact ties half-up: 14.165 → 14.17,
   −5.655 → −5.66 and −0.525 → −0.53. Chapter 5 applies one convention, round-half-up of the exact value, and
   therefore reports **+31.37 pp (exact 31.365, ≈ +31.4)**. The frozen result itself is unchanged; only its
   two-decimal display is.
2. **C10 LeNet-5 P1 − P2 (VAL-RL (I) accuracy AUC).** The exact value is −0.2146, so −0.21. The first draft read −0.22
   from a 3-decimal printout; it was corrected before the freeze and is checked in the audit (P3-lenet).
3. **Frozen corrections from Milestone 1 preserved:**
   - terminal top-10 reach 0% in 5/6 and 5% in 1/6;
   - S2A 80–100%, with 75–100% labelled as S1;
   - +0.89 pp;
   - Phase-5 ResNet-8 mean sparsities 46.2 → 24.5% and 25.9 → 15.6%, with medians labelled as such.
4. **Figure 5.7 content.** The shortlist describes O4 as a "validation vs test feasibility gap". The figure actually
   plots median sparsity against maximum feasible sparsity on VAL-RL. The caption follows the figure.
5. **Figure 5.5.** The Phase-4 F point is plotted at its mean sparsity on an axis labelled "median". This is disclosed;
   it will be corrected in the style-only re-render.
6. **Label in `phase4_statistics.csv`.** The E family's endpoint is labelled `val`, but its values are test
   accuracies: they equal `phase4_baseline_comparison.csv` `ppo_test_mean` and `baseline_test_mean`. Chapter 5
   reports them as test, as the pre-registration defines E.
